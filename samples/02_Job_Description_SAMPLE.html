Battery Systems Engineer

Hybrid, distributed; test work onsite · $115,000-$155,000 base salary + equity

We build home batteries, and the battery team is small: a handful of engineers and one cell partner. You would own a battery subsystem, not a slice of one.

The work is hands-on in the way battery work always is. You will spend more time on what the hardware does on the test rig than on a clean analysis deck. Abuse tests fail, sensors lie, and someone has to find out why before the next build. That is the job, and it is also why people here stay.

What you'll own
  • ·Design and analyze a battery subsystem end to end: what it must do, how it is sized, how it is verified.
  • ·Own cycling and abuse test: build the setup, instrument it, run it, and reduce the data into decisions.
  • ·Thermal and electrical analysis: thermal, FEA, and circuit models the hardware then confirms or refutes.
  • ·Work the interfaces: thermal management, enclosure, firmware, and the one external cell partner. A few weeks a year at the test lab through certification campaigns.
What we need
  • ·Battery pack design in at least one chemistry: LFP, NMC, or another lithium-ion family. You have taken a design from requirements to real hardware.
  • ·Real command of thermal and electrical fundamentals: heat transfer, circuits, and cell behaviour applied to high-energy hardware in production or test settings.
  • ·Hands-on battery test: cycling or abuse-test work, test-rig instrumentation, and turning raw runs into clean data reduction.
  • ·CAD plus analysis fluency: thermal, FEA, or electrical modeling in a standard toolchain, for example ANSYS, MATLAB, or SolidWorks.
  • ·You have owned a subsystem end to end on shipped or test hardware, including the parts nobody scoped, from requirements through verification.
Direct home-storage experience is not required and is not an edge in our screen. The same problem, a large amount of stored energy kept cool and safe in hardware people live with, shows up in EV battery and powertrain work, power electronics, and consumer-electronics battery qualification. We have hired from those fields, and those engineers are among our strongest. If that is you, apply.

Base range reflects the posted location. Compensation also includes equity, health coverage, retirement plan, and paid time off. Open until filled.

We are an equal opportunity employer. All qualified applicants will be considered without regard to any characteristic protected by applicable law. If you need an accommodation to apply or interview, tell your recruiter.

Opening
Team size and the cell partner are in the first two lines. For a small battery team, "own a subsystem, not a slice" is the strongest promise in the post.
Honest cost
The test-rig line is deliberate. Naming that abuse-test and hardware work is hands-on and mostly onsite raises reply quality and kills a late-stage surprise.
Five, not twelve
Long requirement lists suppress applications, including from the adjacent-industry engineers we most want. Everything cuttable was cut to five.
No eligibility gate
This search ships no work-authorization line. Some hardware postings carry one by habit; this role does not, so none was invented or implied.
The closer
The last paragraph converts adjacent-industry engineers, EV powertrain, power electronics, consumer electronics, who would otherwise self-reject. It is the highest-yield sentence in the file.
